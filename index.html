Make a Public Commitment

Define your resolution, rules, reporting frequency, and invite pod peers.

1. Mental Preparation: A powerful goal isn't just a wish—it's a public promise. Be brutally honest with yourself about what you are committing to. Your personal "Why?" will be your anchor when motivation fades.

2. Choose Realistic Frequency: Select a progress reporting frequency (Everyday, Twice a week, etc.) that matches your lifestyle so you can maintain a consistent green streak on your calendar tracker.

3. Leverage Accountability Pods: Invite past peers or share your goal link on WhatsApp to form an accountability pod. Side-by-side progress tracking and constructive advice dramatically increase your success rate.

4. Private Learning Notes: When your journey finishes, your final reflection and takeaways remain permanently private to you forever in your personal history log.
